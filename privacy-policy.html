Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. Introduction

E2FIT ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide when you enquire about or purchase E2FIT healthy food boxes and subscription plans.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we use it, and how you can contact us about your information.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with E2FIT, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Phone or WhatsApp number
  • Email address, if you choose to provide one
  • Delivery address, area and pincode
  • Preferred E2FIT box and subscription plan
  • Preferred delivery period or meal time
  • Information you provide in enquiry forms, advertisements, WhatsApp messages or other communications

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information you provide to:

  • Check delivery availability for your location
  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Recommend or explain suitable E2FIT plans
  • Process and manage orders and subscriptions
  • Arrange delivery and provide customer support
  • Contact you about your enquiry or order
  • Improve our products, services and advertising

4. Advertising and Lead Forms

If you submit an E2FIT lead form through Facebook or Instagram, the information you provide may be received by E2FIT so that we can check delivery availability and contact you about your enquiry.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

5. WhatsApp and Other Service Providers

E2FIT may use third-party platforms such as Meta, WhatsApp, Supabase, website hosting providers and communication tools to operate our website, advertising, lead management, ordering and customer-support processes. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, manage orders and subscriptions, maintain business records, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and protect our legitimate business interests.

8. Your Choices and Rights

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable law and legitimate business requirements. You may also ask us to stop sending promotional communications.

9. Children's Privacy

E2FIT does not knowingly collect personal information from children for advertising or direct marketing purposes. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, technology or legal requirements. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
